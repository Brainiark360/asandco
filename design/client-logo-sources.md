## Presentation and pre-launch checks

- Originals retain their colours and proportions. No grayscale filters, tracing, AI reconstruction, stretching or cropping. Raster images are served through Next Image; SVGs are local original artwork.
- The client strip uses two identical groups for a seamless linear marquee. The duplicate is hidden from assistive technology. A pause/resume button and hover/keyboard-focus pausing stop the motion. Reduced-motion and no-JavaScript users get a single, manually scrollable list; print shows one wrapping list. Original artwork is never animated independently or distorted.
- Confirm permission for public client-logo use before deployment. In particular, [Google's marketing brand guidance](https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google/branding-guidelines/how-to-show-googles-brand/) and [Gates Foundation's grantee/vendor guidance](https://docs.gatesfoundation.org/Documents/GATES_Brand_Guidelines-Grantees_and_Vendors.pdf) require approval for applicable uses. Being a client is not itself proof of logo-use approval. Nothing was published or submitted for approval in this task.
- Obtain GER Holdings' logo and a higher-resolution Iziwacu asset. Confirm the SAC symbol, Tunga regional identity, Avipro's current group mark and secondary-source Kukuyacu artwork with the client before public launch.
- The Work section is still illustrative. No real client name was attached to an invented case study or result.
